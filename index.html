Wide Anchor: Constellation Architect

Connect the stars to make constellations.

Picture this massive star field that just stretches on and on - it's honestly pretty overwhelming at first.

Points of light float across the dark.

Your role is to anchor them - to connect stars into stable constellations.

Not every connection holds.
If it's not balanced, it'll just fall apart.

A Field of Possibility

Each level presents a wide star map.

Stars drift gently in open space.
Some can connect.
Some resist alignment.

Your goal is to form stable constellations by linking stars with anchor lines.

The map is wide.
You need to be really accurate to win.

Core Gameplay

  • 01

    Select a star to begin your constellation.

  • 02

    Draw anchor links between compatible stars.

  • 03

    Ensure connections form a stable pattern.

  • 04

    Avoid unstable crossings or imbalance.

  • 05

    Complete the constellation within structural rules.

Connections remain once placed.

Planning ahead is essential.

Anchor Links

Stars can be connected if they fall within alignment range.

EACH LINK:

  • Must not intersect other links

  • Must respect distance constraints

  • Must contribute to overall balance

Mess up your connections and the whole thing falls apart - learned that the hard way!

Stability System

Every constellation has a stability rating.
Stability depends on:

  • Even distribution of connections

  • Minimal redundancy

  • Balanced structural layout

  • No overloaded stars

Only stable constellations complete the level.

False Alignments

Sometimes stars look like they should connect, but when you try it, the whole thing just doesn't work geometrically.

The map often presents multiple possible formations —
only one configuration achieves full stability.

Efficiency Scoring

You are rewarded for:

  • Minimal anchor usage

  • No unnecessary links

  • Clean geometric balance

  • Optimal structural layout

I swear there's always some better way to do it if you're stubborn enough to keep trying.

Expanding Star Fields

As you progress:

  • Maps become wider

  • Star density increases

  • Link limits apply

  • Multi-constellation challenges appear

  • Moving star systems introduce dynamic puzzles

Things get bigger but it's still easy to follow what's happening.
